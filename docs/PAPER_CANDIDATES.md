# Paper candidates for robot-laboratory tasks

Last screened: **2026-10-07**. Candidate count: **7**.

This is a lightweight discovery list of papers that may support future robot-laboratory task design. Every entry is a **CANDIDATE**, not a fully read source, reviewed task design, converted task, runnable experiment or asset package. Candidates do not change the repository's reviewed-design or task-package counts.

Scope: experimental papers in *Nature*, journals whose full titles begin with *Nature*, or *Science*. **npj journals are excluded.** This batch focuses on nonbiological mechanics, materials, fabrication and measurement.

## Screening and access limits

- All seven linked official publisher pages were opened on the screening date. Titles, journal names, publication years and DOIs were checked; abstracts and selected Methods passages were screened for concrete experimental work
- **OA** means the publisher labels the article Open Access. Main HTML access does not establish access to every supplement or permission to reuse every source component
- Complete papers, PDFs, supplementary information, movies, code and raw datasets have **not** been fully reviewed for this list. No paper-level completeness or reproducibility claim is made
- Suitability descriptions are preliminary inferences. They identify possible task structure, not extracted operating procedures or evidence that a robot can perform the work
- Exact DOIs and titles were checked against the existing repository and previously accepted task-design inventory to avoid duplicate candidate entries
- The entangled-network and polymer-inspired-metamaterials pages list **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**. Their inclusion is a citation and discovery record, not permission to adapt or redistribute their source content. Other source-component rights require their own check before reuse

Further source review and task conversion are separate work. This list adds no operating parameters, biological-engineering procedures, 3D assets or experimental execution claims.
