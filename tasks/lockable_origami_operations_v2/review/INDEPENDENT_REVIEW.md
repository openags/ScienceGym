Prepared specimens require ancestry and earn no fabrication credit. Coupons take a separate cut-only route and never acquire origami fold/bond/reconfiguration steps.

The four quantitative compression branches retain three independent samples per mode, while density scaling retains three sizes and five independent samples at each size with MD normalization. Directional A=45 degrees, B=90 degrees and z have separate mount/area/history requirements. All eleven source problems remain visible and branch-local, including table color/count scope, N18 angle, bar counts, different layer roles, density equations, angle conventions, the N6 modulus-ratio discrepancy, causal cycle references, the SI biaxial ratio and the ambiguous perforation interval. No disputed equation, source outcome or displayed scene dimension becomes an executable control or exact experimental target.
