## Geometry and fabrication lineage

Declare an original even-sided primitive, face adjacency, cut/fold assignment, nominal a, b and phi, mountain/valley convention, in-plane lattice vectors and layer map. Source demonstrators use N=4,6,8,10,12; typical physical a=b=15 mm and phi=60 degrees. Density experiments use 10,15,20 mm; mixed-mode arrays use 10 mm. A source (7,7) tessellation along lattice vectors is not automatically forty-nine complete untrimmed polygon rings on Cartesian axes. Exact cut/fold CAD, edge clipping, bond masks and sample boundaries remain externally qualified inputs.

Register cellulose-paperboard lot, individual sheets, machine direction (MD), cross direction (CD), condition and uncertainty. The source nominal material is 200 g/m2 and 0.21 mm dry thickness. Cutting requests go to a closed qualified laser service and return independent inspection evidence; the source 2 mm cut and 1.4 mm interval wording does not establish a gap or pitch. No laser power, speed, toolpath or safe machine limit is supplied.

For origami assemblies, verify a partial fold before mirrored stacking and triangular mating-face bonding with identified PVA. Record every sheet and layer ID, layer order, adhesive lot, bond map, alignment, independent bond inspection and a qualified cure/release record. Missing glue amount, cure conditions, tolerances and acceptance thresholds are gates. Nothing here asserts that a rendered fold or an acknowledged service produced an intact assembly. Replacements create new specimen and layer identities; failed attempts remain archived.

The base-material tensile route branches from the parent paperboard sheet into separately identified MD/CD coupons. It does not fold or bond these coupons. Source coupons are 240 by 5 mm with 30 mm grip regions on each end, a 180 mm crosshead gauge and a separate 20 mm DIC gauge. Those two strain supports must not be conflated. Independent measured dimensions, grip/marker calibration, compliance correction and repeat schedules remain required.

Prepared-specimen entry needs independently verified upstream ancestry and never earns fabrication credit. A synthetic FULL route checks recorded preparation lineage only. Destructive or yielded/cycled samples cannot be silently reused for virgin first-cycle comparisons. Folded or reconfigured specimens are not automatically pristine after apparent visual recovery.

## Configuration, contact and transport

Configuration records distinguish developed, partial, bifurcation, regular-mode, flat-folded and compressed-contact states. A requested mode, rendered accordion, rubber band or unverified photograph is not proof of locking. Contact-supported rigidity is conditional on compression; this is not an autonomous latch or guaranteed unloaded retention. Single-chain irregular modes cannot be promoted into tessellated arrays.

The source has three different layer-count roles: physical proof-of-concept even-layer choices; theoretical single-DoF minima 3/3/5/5/5 for N4/6/8/10/12; and locked-rigidity minima 1/2/3/3/3 under the source contact assumption. Quantitative N4/N6 compression uses six layers. These roles remain separate.

An authored carrier supports identified sheets/coupons/assemblies through custody transfers. Before mounting, independently inspect identity, condition, mode, orientation and branch geometry. Mounting creates a new revision and invalidates stale mount-dependent observations. Reconfiguration similarly changes its revision and invalidates dependent area, mode and readout evidence. Carrier, fixture, grasp and camera geometry in the paired scene are original unqualified display geometry. No asset certifies robot reach, clearance, self-contact, fit, kinematics, friction, deformation or stiffness.

## Experimental branches and controls

The visually verified planar grid is (1,1),(3,3),(5,5),(7,7),(9,9), and the stacking grid is n=1,2,4,6,8. Five independent samples per geometry are reported. Full primitive-cell effective-area conventions, rather than a guessed outer chain footprint, define stress.

The four first-cycle compression routes remain N4 A2, N4 AO, N6 A3 and N6 A2O, with three independent specimens per source curve group. Density scaling has five independent specimens at each of the three a=b values and uses machine-direction base material normalization. Samples, cycles, force time points and camera frames are four different counting units.

N4 A2 cycling contains ten source cycles; N6 A3 cycling contains four. The source reversal at approximately 75 percent of first-cycle peak needs a frozen, independent reference or conditioning plan before the request. Future observations cannot be used to justify an earlier control action. Residual set and progressive damage stay in sample history. Source qualitative stabilization and reported residual strain are literature context, never task targets or fixture acceptance limits.

The multidirectional comparison records in-plane A at 45 degrees and B at 90 degrees relative to specimen x, and out-of-plane z. Each direction has its own mounted axes, effective area/height, sample/damage allocation and uncertainty. Replicate counts absent from the source remain required bounded episode inputs rather than assumed one.

Seven N4 mixed-mode configurations need independently supplied spatial maps and image masks. Exact source cell assignments are not available as discrete CAD/data, and the source figure is not digitized into fabrication geometry. Modulus is normalized to configuration 1; open-channel area is normalized to a positive configuration 4 baseline. The denominators cannot substitute for each other. Open-channel area is a geometric descriptor; the paper does not demonstrate measured flow permeability.

The retained-band versus removal-under-preload control is a distinct closed-service route. The historical removal context is approximately 2 percent of peak load; a reported illustrative force is not a safety limit. Require a qualified external removal method, independently established baseline, observed preload and post-release band-state record. No robot cutting under load is implemented.

Movies 4–10 are numerical animations, including biaxial force-ratio examples, and cannot supply physical machine observations. Movies 11–12 and corresponding human/book load demonstrations remain source-only non-executable context. They are not converted into robot payload goals or safe person-support tests.

## Measurements, readouts and safe closure

Before a load request, independently verify current mount, guarding/interlock, force range, displacement/DIC calibration, clock alignment, environment, uncertainty and branch analysis plan. Named source apparatus, 12.5 kN load-cell capacity, 10^-3 per-second strain rate, 22 degrees C and approximately 30 percent RH are reported context, not operating authorization or safety thresholds.

Independent records contain force, time, crosshead displacement, registered DIC/image identities and current sample/lot/design/layer/configuration/mount/calibration/control/axis/baseline revisions. Requests and acknowledgements never become sensor observations. Missing, altered, nonfinite, mis-united, out-of-range, stale or cross-specimen records fail closed.

Engineering stress uses declared initial effective area; strain uses the matching initial height or gauge. The engaged linear interval is declared explicitly and compression fits remain within the source maximum strain 0.05. The first qualified local peak before densification defines the source yield observable, not the global high-strain maximum. The helper is deliberately bounded to unambiguous synthetic peaks; real noise, plateaus and detection thresholds require qualified input. Crosshead and DIC strains stay separate until a registered comparison.

Cyclic reduction preserves chronological start/turn/end segments, signed open-path work and residual set. Nonclosed paperboard curves are not forced into closed hysteresis loops. A positive baseline and exact requested count are mandatory. Mean and sample standard deviation are calculated across unique independent specimen IDs, never time points.

Request shutdown, then independently verify current no-load/isolation before undocking. Unknown isolation remains contained. Post-release inspection determines storage or quarantine; damaged specimens and dependent descendants cannot regain virgin status. Archive raw and derived evidence, failures and holds. Cleanup records station/tool/custody closure only; no physical cleanup or machine adapter is implemented.
