# Bianisotropic acoustic metasurfaces: whole-paper robot task design

## Deliverable and scientific scope

This is an original, source-grounded task design for Li et al., *Systematic design and experimental demonstration of bianisotropic metasurfaces for scattering-free manipulation of acoustic wavefronts*, Nature Communications 9, 1342 (2018), DOI [10.1038/s41467-018-03778-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03778-9). The supplied evidence packet contains the complete nine-page published main article and required 24-page Supplementary Information. The optional peer-review file is not inspected. Data availability on main page 8 directs requests to the authors; raw pressure records, author CAD/STL, solver projects and optimization code are absent.

The task asks whether a reciprocal, geometrically asymmetric acoustic panel can redirect incident normal energy flux into a chosen transmitted direction while suppressing parasitic scattering, and how this depends on local geometry, spatial sampling, loss and architectural constraints. It preserves the connection between theoretical requirements, local-cell design, numerical controls, fabrication, assembly, instrument qualification, complete acquisition and interpretation. It is not an angle lookup or an action sequence that emits the paper's efficiencies.

There are 42 operation templates and 14 selectable branches. Source coverage is represented by 12 families, with the complete main-figure, Methods, eight-SI-figure, three-table and eight-note inventory in `coverage_matrix.json`. Every operation has an actor, station, objects, tool interface, actions, observable postconditions, provenance, missing-input gates and recovery contract. A branch's operation list is membership, not a mandatory total ordering. Repeated templates receive occurrence IDs with branch, run, configuration, specimen/version and iteration context.

The paper's only reported physical angle is 60 degrees (main Experimental measurements, page 6). The 70- and 80-degree designs are numerical; so are ideal phase-only generalized-Snell's-law (GSL) comparisons, viscous-loss sensitivity and three-resonator comparisons. SI Note 3, pages 17–18, uses four pressure probes and two terminations in COMSOL. It does not report a separate physical four-microphone impedance-tube experiment. Focusing, cloaking and arbitrary-field applications in Discussion remain proposals.

## Independent computational branches

1. `THEORY_TARGET`: declare coordinates, pressure phasors, port normals and the desired incident/transmitted directions; derive a target impedance profile and normal-flux constraints. A reciprocal local cell has equal forward/backward transmission. Different reflection phases are compatible with reciprocity. Structural asymmetry, complete target control at fixed thickness, and the practical choice of four resonators are distinct claims (main pages 3–4; SI Notes 1–2).
2. `LOCAL_BIDIRECTIONAL`: compare the analytic transfer-matrix response of the four-side-cavity architecture to a separately computed numerical response for the same geometry, medium, frequency schedule and convention. Retain complex matrices and residuals, not only magnitude curves (main Methods, page 7; SI Note 5 and Figure 6).
3. `RETRIEVAL_NUMERICAL`: configure four distinct numerical pressure probes with a cell datum, then run plane-wave-radiation and hard-wall terminations. Require eight complex pressure values per frequency, independent jobs, documented port signs and a nonsingular conditioned inversion. A coincident/aliased probe layout or repeated termination is not successful retrieval (SI Note 3, Figure 4).
4. `GA_60`, `GA_70`, `GA_80`: retain the published baseline search settings: population 10, mutation rate 0.2, retain half, keep the best candidate unmutated, no crossover, stop after 1500 generations and perform 50 restarts per cell. SI Figure 7 shows ten illustrative traces for the first 60-degree cell; this is not a reduction of the fifty-restart procedure. Cost normalization, full bounds, mutation distribution and seeds require qualified authored inputs. The 70/80-degree designs use COMSOL Livelink with MATLAB for impedance evaluation. The later suggested local refinement with crossover is an optional extension, not baseline GA (main Genetic algorithm, page 7; SI Note 6).
5. `NUM_60`, `NUM_70`, `NUM_80`: build the source's eleven-cell 60-degree period or four-cell 70/80-degree periods and a matched-resolution ideal impedance-matched GSL control. Use identical illumination, frequency, medium, domain/PML policy, convergence policy and flux normalization within each pair. Preserve analytic target, optimized geometry and solver outputs as different artifacts (main Figure 3, pages 4–5; SI Tables 1–3).
6. `NUM_LOSS_60`: compare matching 60-degree geometry with lossless and viscous-fluid models. Main page 8 gives dynamic viscosity 1.82 × 10^-5 Pa s and bulk viscosity 5.46 × 10^-2 Pa s. SI Note 4 omits seconds and points to the wrong figure; use main Methods units and the actual SI Figure 5, with the discrepancy recorded.
7. `NUM_TOPOLOGY`: preserve both the constrained three-resonator failed search and the separately relaxed-neck comparison. SI Note 8 keeps cell width 12 mm, shell thickness 1 mm, neck width 1.5 mm and cell length 50 mm for the constrained comparison, and reports no solution within its stated 20% error criterion. The relaxed branch narrows neck width to 0.6 mm and varies neck heights; its reported cost is 4.47%. These are references to finite searches, not universal impossibility proofs. The 50 mm length is scoped to that comparison and is not silently promoted into complete author CAD.

Computational jobs have no fabrication or physical-calibration prerequisite. A qualified source-table-geometry route and a reoptimized-geometry route are alternatives. A blocked printer cannot invalidate source review or a qualified independent solver job. Conversely, a complete numerical job does not establish a fabricated experiment. This package specifies the jobs but implements no acoustic solver, optimizer or physics engine.

## Source dimensions and geometry limits

The 60-degree design operates at 3000 Hz with an approximately 132 mm period and eleven 12 mm-wide cells. Target sampling uses x_n = (n − 0.375)w to avoid singular sample locations (main page 5). SI Table 1 provides eleven local-cell channel/cavity dimensions and rounded resonance/scattering values. Tables 2 and 3 provide four local designs for each larger angle. All 19 rows are preserved in `geometry_tables.json` as factual transcription, not CAD or raw measurements. The unusual Table 1 row 4 reflection entry, 0.31 + 0.028i, is retained.

Those tables do not specify full three-dimensional geometry, extrusion/guide height, joining, sealing, fixture interfaces or complete cavity-length values. The 0.1 mm cavity entries particularly require a real process/manufacturability review. This package does not silently fill absent dimensions, infer nozzle settings, create collision meshes or repair printed coefficients. Selected geometry-only values may enter a qualified input card; the attached source scattering, cost and resonance outputs remain evaluator/author reference context.

## Physical preparation and sample custody

`PREPARE_60` connects fabrication to an inspectable nine-period panel. Main page 8 reports fused-deposition-modeling ABS, density 1180 kg/m³ and sound speed 2700 m/s, treating the walls as acoustically rigid relative to air. It gives no printer/nozzle/layer/infill/support/extraction recipe or dimensional acceptance tolerance.

The proposed robot retrieves identified ABS stock and an empty build plate, checks lot and carrier labels, and transports retained items through supplied safe routes. At the enclosed print service it seats the plate, loads stock through the qualified interface, verifies geometry and process readbacks, closes the guard and clears the motion zone. The device performs printing. The robot waits for separate job-complete and thermal/motion-safe-release receipts, then retrieves the supported plate and separates parts using a qualified extraction method. A completion flag alone never permits touching a hot or moving build.

Each section receives a physical ID linked to stock, build job and geometry revision. At the inspection cradle, the robot checks accessible necks, straight channels, shell condition, local-cell order and supplied dimensional datums. Blockage, broken walls or uncertain inaccessible features lead to rejection or quarantine. No improvised scraping, heating, flushing or acoustic-performance claim follows from visual acceptance. Failed builds and replacements retain their own identities.

The authored handling granularity is one complete period section. This is a benchmark choice: the source does not establish how many separate printed parts were handled. The robot stages nine distinct accepted sections in numbered positions and matches their incident faces, tangential direction and eleven ordered design positions. Nine periods imply 99 local design slots, not 99 independently fabricated specimens. The robot supports the sections, seats and secures them through qualified clamps/seals, then inspects seams, flatness, aperture and orientation. Every change creates a new panel assembly version and invalidates older assembly inspection.

Independent fabricated sample count remains unknown. Four acquisitions at a scan point are repeated measurements of one specimen/position, not four independent samples. The allocation card determines any authored campaign count, reuse and replacement policy without claiming it reproduces a historical count.

## Station qualification and measurement

`QUALIFY_GUIDE` and `MEASURE_60` expose setup work that a protocol summary often hides. The proposed scene includes records/computation, stock/carriers, enclosed fabrication, inspection, assembly, calibration, a two-dimensional waveguide, storage/quarantine and cleanup. Every physical station transition needs supported transport, a clear route, detached safe tethers and an available destination dock. Robot grasp frames, reachability, mass limits and routes are not built here.

The robot inspects the supplied guide configuration with source off and stage stopped, registers sample/source/scan coordinates and checks microphone clearance. Guide plate gap, materials, absorbers, fastening, sealing, source-to-sample distance and scan height are qualified environment inputs, not recovered source facts. It connects identified speaker, microphone and DAQ channels with outputs isolated and routes strain relief outside scan paths.

The source reports 28 loudspeakers producing a normally incident Gaussian-modulated beam. Speaker make, spacing, drive level, beam width, channel envelope and exposure limits are missing. The robot/device pair must read back a qualified waveform and source card rather than inventing them. Microphone/DAQ amplitude and phase calibration, a reference artifact, validity interval, uncertainty, phase/time reference and source drift checks are authored benchmark validity controls; the paper does not provide their recipe.

The published overall scan extent is 114 by 60 cm with 2 cm steps and four measurements at each point (main page 8). Exact origin, endpoints, point inclusion, reflection-region allocation and the analysis-line offset are not given. `SCAN_PLAN` therefore requires explicit nonempty point sets and timing cards. It does not compute a paper-reported point count from endpoint assumptions. Sampling rate, record length, window, trigger, stage settling and height remain gated.

For a reflection reference the robot verifies that the guide is empty without disturbing the registered coordinate system. For each scheduled blank point it positions the microphone, checks pose and settling, and causes the instrument to acquire four separately identified records. Pose, phase/calibration/configuration, clipping and acquisition status are recorded. A failed record remains in the ledger; a retry receives a new raw ID. Complete scan status requires every declared point with four valid distinct acquisitions. Missing values never become zero pressure.

After a complete matched blank reflection scan, the robot disables the source and parks the probe, carries the supported qualified panel into the guide, matches incident face and datums, clamps/seals it and rechecks coordinates. `RUN_CHECK` authorizes sample-transmission and sample-reflection scan occurrences only while panel version, installation epoch, geometry, calibration and source drift remain compatible. Removing and reinstalling the panel creates a new install epoch. A moved frame, changed DAQ settings or source drift invalidates the old blank pairing. Re-referencing may require removing the sample, acquiring a new empty-guide field and reinstalling/reacquiring the sample field.

## Reduction, controls and interpretation

The temporal Fourier operation converts acquired records to calibrated complex pressure. Four-record averaging is tied to a declared phase reference and processing card, with all parent records retained. Complex sample-reflection field minus complex empty-guide field gives the reflected field; subtracting magnitudes is not an equivalent operation. Both fields must share grid, guide/frame, source calibration, microphone, DAQ configuration, phase reference, medium and frequency signatures. A closing drift check must pass before final validity; otherwise the pair is retained as invalid and new matched acquisition is required.

Spatial Fourier analysis uses the declared near-panel line and retains its offset, window, wave-vector mapping and normalization. The paper labels the desired transmitted direction as the −1 order. Near-field evanescent modes can be visible in the spectrum; their amplitude must not be treated as propagating normal energy flux. Reflected pressure amplitude, pressure magnitude-squared maps and normal-flux efficiency are different quantities, particularly at oblique angles (main pages 5–6).

The comparison ledger keeps four distinctions explicit:

- Numerical incident-to-target efficiency is reported as 93%, 96% and 91% for the three lossless bianisotropic designs
- The 60-degree discretized GSL numerical result is 81% in the abstract/Results, while 89% is the theoretical upper bound; Discussion lists 89% alongside numerical comparison values. Both remain, with the inconsistency recorded as `SC01`
- The reported experimental 97% is a share of transmitted energy directed as intended, not 97% of incident energy
- The approximately 2% reflected-energy statement is rounded; the introduction says less than 2%. No missing normalization/calibration detail is invented to make a stronger measurement claim

These values are source reference evidence and never automatic success thresholds. The evaluator accepts a procedurally valid, honestly reported measurement that differs from the literature. It cannot accept selecting a figure, geometry row or angle as evidence that a pressure field was acquired.

The seven control packages cover three angle-matched ideal GSL pairs, the loss pair, topology comparison, empty/sample reflection pair and authored pre/post drift validity. Reversed assembly, blocked necks, wrong order and gaps are proposed adversarial faults, not extra paper experiments with invented numerical responses.

## Safe closure, gaps and evaluation

The robot stops acquisition, disables speaker outputs, confirms safe readbacks, parks the microphone, supports the panel before unclamping, removes it along a supplied path and records storage/quarantine custody. Tools and calibration artifacts return to identified holders; loose print remnants and waste are segregated under qualified rules; stations remain isolated. A physical campaign cannot close with missing output-off, custody or cleanup evidence.

All raw records and failed attempts are append-only. Derived fields point to raw parents, calibration, processing and source/condition revisions. Numerical records retain geometry, solver, mesh/PML/source settings, convergence and objective lineage. Reports separately state source completeness, task-design completeness, actual procedural execution, observed science and limitations.

The 18 unknown groups cover complete geometry, printer/process settings, independent samples, guide/fixtures, source, microphone/DAQ/calibration, grids, solver settings, air/medium, search definitions, mathematical conventions, unavailable original raw data, tolerances/uncertainty, source rounding, optional peer review, robot assets, trusted event infrastructure and safe recovery/cleanup. Unknowns have no default values. They block their own dependency closures. Missing historical raw data blocks historical replay, not the archiving of new qualified observations. Optional unread peer review is not represented as an execution gate.

The actor may receive its selected goal, current inventory, selected factual cards, qualified operating inputs and observations already acquired. It may not receive reference routes, literature outcome tables, hidden fault schedules, future records, optimization winners or evaluator predicates. The repository includes these author/evaluator files for review; they must not be mounted wholesale into a runtime actor context. A minimal static whitelist projection test exists, but a typed production card loader and trusted runtime evaluator are not implemented.

The standard-library suite checks structure and synthetic bookkeeping, including adversarial mutations of part identity/order, stale inspection, hot builds, incomplete or duplicated acquisitions, phase/frame/source/DAQ mismatch, invalid blank subtraction, denominator confusion, unconverged numerical controls, singular retrieval, missing GA restarts, actor leakage and skipped cleanup. The retrieval fixture additionally checks the actual transformed interface matrix using pressure and impedance-normalized velocity, so conditioning is not inferred from probe matrices separately. These small synthetic matrix calculations are not acoustic simulation. The supplied fixture validator does not establish that a device produced a record or that pressure values are physically correct. Robot geometry, CAD, acoustic simulation, hardware execution and scientific replication remain unvalidated.
