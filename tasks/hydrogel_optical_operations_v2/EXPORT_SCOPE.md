# Export scope

Only files named in EXPORT_ALLOWLIST.json may be copied. They are original English task metadata, authored contracts, documentation, synthetic tests and review receipts. The allowlist hashes every payload except itself; no wildcard paths or symlinks are permitted.

Publisher PDFs, main/SI/Extended Data images, videos, workbooks, source dumps and their derivatives are not exportable. Source URLs and factual hashes in provenance do not authorize redistribution of the referenced bytes. Source source-access/readiness limitations remain mandatory in every integration.

Actor visibility is restricted to agent_visible.json. Other files are for authoring and evaluator review. Passing synthetic checks does not establish source completeness, apparatus qualification, physical simulation, execution or scientific replication. This local package performs no publication or external write.
