## Limits and release boundary

The checker is a deterministic predicate over declared synthetic metadata and returns no hardware command. Invented positive fixtures demonstrate the bookkeeping rules only. They do not authenticate a service, establish actual calibration, resolve physical unknowns or authorize equipment. Public evaluator material does not implement a secure hidden actor/evaluator split. The finite checker operates one scoped specimen episode; whole-study cross-specimen aggregation and independent replication are not implemented. Illustrative assets and receipt identifiers are not qualified grasps, robot poses, scientific observations or genuine safety evidence.

The original task text and finite export guards are accepted within those limits. Exact final task ZIP bytes, scene artifacts and the task/scene pair seal are separate finalization checks. This report intentionally contains no self-referential package hash and does not certify an archive that may be created later. After any change, rerun both suites and structural verification, then freeze the exact allowlist and record actual ZIP/pair results in detached receipts.
