# Task design and scientific boundary

## Objective

An embodied benchmark agent organizes evidence and protected specimen custody for an inert, suspended acoustic scattering experiment. It selects named symbolic operations and existing evaluator evidence IDs. It cannot set acoustic drive, exposure, optical alignment, source trajectories, manufacturing settings, force constants or robot poses. The agent succeeds by preserving traceability, controls, failures and closeout; agreement with published outcomes is not a success criterion.

The paper reports three fabricated groove design families on a torsion pendulum. M1 supports lateral response and a pattern-flip comparison; M2 supports one-axis following of a changing source; M3 supports angle-dependent attractive/repulsive displacement. No free-flight levitation, biological work, multi-axis robot actuation or coiled physical specimen is asserted.

## Full preparation and dependency chain

R01 registers the final main/SI hashes, written and visual coverage and the unresolved-input ledger. A reviewed paper does not qualify apparatus. R02 requires a prospective approved plan for independent specimens, mounting epochs, trials, condition order, settling, exclusions, uncertainty and acceptance. Every missing count or threshold remains null rather than being recovered from a plot.

R03 reviews a closed fabrication/QA release for an inert specimen, its unique identity, design family, revision, batch, condition and protected supported carrier. Rejected or obstructed specimens remain in the record. M1/M2/M3 labels are families, not three assumed physical replicates; M3 cannot inherit M1 dimensions. R04 reviews individual array-member selection, rejected elements, membership revision, current aggregate characterization and drift. No voltage-to-pressure or exposure-safety inference is allowed.

R05 accepts service custody only with receiver acceptance, exclusive lease, safe access and support occupancy. The old holder remains authoritative until the new holder accepts. R06 reviews current optical, torsion, sign and clock qualification bound to the actual specimen, mount, array, optics and configuration. The source's historical sensitivity is not calibration. Rod length does not establish the effective force lever. A sign-tested mapping must distinguish camera axes, pendulum displacement, laboratory coordinates and source angle.

## Experimental branch loop

Every B branch instantiates R07 admission, R08 observed records, R09 control/analysis review and R10 continuation. Branch inventory is not a forced scientific order. All branches require preparation, current calibration, independent repeat units and approved scoped condition plans.

- B01: retain distinct source-off baseline, source-on response and post-off recovery phases. Independently observed emission events establish phase transitions. A return toward initial image coordinates is not a safe-access release.
- B02: preserve every prospective source-condition label, condition result, nonresponse, failed attempt and exclusion. Supplied electrical conditions cannot substitute for measured acoustic output or safety qualification.
- B03: pair the same specimen's opposite orientations through separate mounts and calibrations. Require actual orientation records and an approved signed comparison transform. A flip invalidates affected qualification. No flat unpatterned physical control is invented.
- B04: pair actual source-position readback with camera-derived response using separate units and coordinate signs. Commands and movies cannot supply position, clock alignment, physical time, lag or tracking accuracy. Preserve the one-axis and suspended-object scope.
- B05: retain the complete approved source-angle inventory with observed pose and uncertainty. Do not reward a transition at the paper's reported angle, choose only nearby samples, or rename normalized numerical force as measured newtons.

A planned condition without an observed result is incomplete, not a zero. A failed attempt remains part of denominator/accounting and retains its raw data. A new attempt gets a new ID. Uncertain external admission requires authoritative status reconciliation; blind duplicate requests are prohibited.

## Numerical source coverage

R11 provides four read-only source-review arms, independent of physical admission: A01 stress-tensor/prism comparison; A02 groove phase and analytical/optimized design comparisons; A03 coiled topology alternatives; A04 beam-width sensitivity. No solver or physics run occurs. Two coiled tables are numerical alternatives, not additional fabricated specimens. Beam-width comparisons use source normalization and show a nonmonotonic peak trend; they are not a physical beam-width sweep. The M2 numerical illustration uses a different spacing from the experiment. Centered symmetry implies zero lateral force in the source model, not zero total acoustic force.

All five parameter tables and 152 entries were reviewed. This task preserves table inventory, count, provenance and value-list hashes rather than using those values to reconstruct source CAD. Scene geometry is deliberately original and illustrative.

## Cross-device measurement lineage

Raw camera pixels, screen displacement, pendulum angle, specimen displacement and inferred force are distinct nodes. Each derived node requires parent identifiers and hashes, units, frame/sign tests, uncertainty and current calibration. Baseline subtraction names both raw parents. Filtering and window selection retain their policy and excluded intervals. Force inference remains held without a separately qualified torsion/geometry transformation; displacement-only evidence must not imply measured force.

Device clocks have separate identities, synchronization receipts, residual error, scope and invalidation conditions. Dynamic rate/lag claims remain held when alignment is unknown. Source angle in degrees, response displacement in length units, normalized force and force per length remain distinct.

## Failure, containment and closeout

Any identity mismatch, stale receipt, lost tracking, drift, clock break, support problem, incomplete condition set or unknown service status creates a hold. Retain the accepted custody holder and exclusive lease, preserve partial data, and seek qualified containment under a separately approved facility process. Synthetic state transitions are never actual containment commands.

R12 requires four independent, current observed safety records: acoustic off, optical beam safe, motion safe and support safe. The records must cover the actual configuration and include independently qualified safe-access authority. A stop request, timeout, disabled UI, service completion or scientific trace cannot supply these records.

R13 requires a supported handoff with from/to holder, specimen, slot, condition and accepted receiver. Return and quarantine are valid distinct outcomes. Unknown holder or occupancy prevents normal closeout. No autonomous cleaning, disposal or unsupported suspended pickup is defined. R14 seals every trial, attempt, failure, exclusion, calibration and custody transition. Branch completion is not session completion.

## Evaluation and trust boundary

The Python contract is a small offline finite evidence-bookkeeping checker. Each synthetic experimental episode admits exactly one specimen identity. Whole-study cross-specimen aggregation is not implemented. Prospective multi-specimen study design remains a held external requirement; this finite checker rejects more than one specimen-level repeat in an episode. Separate design/branch episodes can be catalogued at the paper level without claiming one combined physical campaign. Numerical contexts permit source registration and R11 review only. Actor actions contain only operation and evidence IDs. Evaluator-owned records are immutable copies; self-reported booleans or actor-injected measurement values cannot qualify an attempt. Synthetic issuers demonstrate role separation only. Cryptographic service authentication, real instrument integration and an inaccessible hidden evaluator are not implemented.

Positive fixtures contain explicit invented synthetic approvals solely to exercise the checker. They do not resolve any of U01–U16, do not modify the real-world default HOLD_QUALIFICATION and do not turn illustrative anchors into qualified robot poses. A hash validates byte identity, not scientific truth or physical safety.

## Source interpretation and unknowns

Eight source ambiguities/scope distinctions remain explicit: design-specific envelope; two figure-reference issues; SD/confidence wording; torsion lever; optical transfer; self-guiding coordinate polarity; simulation/experiment spacing. Sixteen unknown groups cover raw data, fabrication, geometry, array, metrology, repeats, timing, exact plans, exposure qualification, support, custody, closeout, numerical configuration, signs and update status. A later source-update receipt may narrow U16 without claiming a comprehensive publisher status audit.

The package is one design regardless of branch count. Independent review, sanitized export and the exact pair seal establish only the released design artifact. They do not establish physical execution, safe operation or reproduction.
