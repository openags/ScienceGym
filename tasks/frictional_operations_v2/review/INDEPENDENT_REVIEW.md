## Runtime and adversarial findings

The actor proposal surface admits only an operation ID and an evaluator-supplied evidence ID. Plans and receipts are copied on admission. Tests check exact field sets, campaign/epoch identity, plan/semantic digests, dependency hashes, recorded rejection chains, bounded digital retries, immutable returned copies, and non-mutating rejection behavior.

R11 is genuinely per registered job. A preparation job can close before instrument qualification, measurement branches or aggregate analysis. Each closeout binds the exact originating receipt hash and subject, and now reports only its registered job's branch scope. Failed jobs must remain quarantined; unstarted and reviewed jobs cannot exchange disposition meanings. R12 requires every primary route disposition, every registered job closeout, all preceding rejected-event hashes, all branch dispositions and all twenty qualification holds.

The following independently reproduced defects were corrected and regression-tested before acceptance:

1. Incomplete or mis-typed comparison plans could omit the low/high partner or assign inappropriate branch roles. Explicit low/high and baseline/altered roles plus authored matched-pair identities now constrain the plan. Statistical repeat groups remain a separate concept.
2. Grain and host lot IDs could alias, and one dispersion batch could acquire incompatible host parents. Both contradictions now reject.
3. A spent state could reuse another preparation's settled state or its own loaded state. The protected preparation-state identity set is now checked.
4. Requested and independently observed condition references could be identical. This alias now rejects.
5. Derived evidence IDs could repeat across runs. Duplicate derived IDs and raw/derived identity aliases now reject.
6. Service jobs could carry the wrong job kind, and a reviewed job could close as not started. Both now reject.
7. Malformed plan rows leaked ordinary Python exceptions. They now reject through the contract error interface.
8. The exporter admitted non-finite JSON values and leaked exceptions for malformed record/member shapes. Non-finite and overflowing floats reject, and explicit structural checks return export errors.
9. A truncated route-binding list could bypass the tail of a zip-based validation loop. Exact binding inventory and semantic inventory/flag checks now reject that case even after a self-consistent rehash.

The producer also strengthened CR01-CR09 control links and per-job branch scopes during review. Independent tests verify omission, evidence-class promotion, swapped condition scope and broad closeout-branch promotion are rejected.
