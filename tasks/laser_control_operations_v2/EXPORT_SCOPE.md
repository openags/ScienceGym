# Export scope

This exact allowlist contains original task design, source attribution, original synthetic evaluator code, tests and reviews only. Only `agent_visible.json` is actor context; every other exported file is evaluator/audit context. The audit ZIP is not an actor observation package.

Excluded: retained article XML/PDF, extracted source prose, source figures or renders, peer-review documents, author code, raw scientific data, source CAD/FEM files, local paths, caches, logs and credentials. CC BY 4.0 attribution does not clear separately credited material or third-party software. No such assets are included.

Design coverage is complete at the paper level. Verified behavior is only the bounded synthetic contract and arithmetic. No physical run, simulator, fabrication, hazard qualification or real safety controller is provided. Export hashes and exact members demonstrate byte integrity, not scientific truth or durable authentication.
