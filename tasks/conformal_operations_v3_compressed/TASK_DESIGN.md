# Conformal elasticity: whole-paper task design

## Scope and source boundaries
This package represents one paper: Czajkowski, Coulais, van Hecke and Rocklin, Conformal elasticity of mechanism-based metamaterials, Nature Communications 13, 211 (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-27825-0. Its sixteen operations do not become sixteen papers. The whole-paper design has nine branches: B01 material/fixture preparation, B02 foot loading, B03 bridge loading, B04 periodic-cell FEM, B05 full-structure FEM, B06 separate fitting/inference analyses, B07 analytical theory, B08 spring-network boundary control and B09 comparison/closeout. Only B02 and B03 are physical experiment branches. B04, B05, B07 and B08 remain documented source scope, unexecuted. B06 distinguishes physical evidence from numerical source evidence.

The accepted upstream review read all nine main pages, all sixteen written SI pages with ten notes and six figures, and the media-description sheet. Three movies decoded successfully and five frames per movie were visually sampled. This is not continuous visual or quantitative movie analysis. Zenodo v1 metadata and its six-file listing were read; the archives were not inspected. That preprint-era version is not verified to correspond exactly to final article panels. No source software is executed here.

Twenty-eight source facts, original translation requirements, nominal asset geometry, unknown inputs, source conflicts and arbitrary synthetic test records remain separate provenance classes. The main/SI source material is not bundled. Apparatus/material names are historical attribution, not current procurement recommendations or qualification receipts.

## Preparation, specimen lineage and custody
The route starts before a specimen exists. R01 requires a frozen prospective campaign policy. R02 commissions a qualified fabrication service and binds beam/pad/foot material lots to independent CAD and pad-layout revisions, print jobs, postprocess, age and inspection records. Agilus 30, Vero Black and ABS source identifications do not supply a complete safe fabrication recipe. This task contains no resin preparation, synthesis or machine controls. A service request cannot stand in for completed and inspected parts. Integrated pads remain linked to their beam, while the rigid foot has its own identity.

R03 requires both fixture drawings and independent contact/mount inspection. The scalar beam envelope, 48-by-10 square count, square/pad sizes and hinge thickness do not uniquely recover exact CAD, physical pretwist, fillets or pad placement. Foot shape, bridge support span and contact/pusher radii remain unresolved. A nominal scene cannot close these gaps.

R04 binds contact preparation to specimen state and branch. The reported fine powder belongs explicitly to the foot branch; its identity, amount, application and cleanup are unreported. No unidentified powder is handled. Any treatment requires an independently approved closed-service method, material identity and safety review. The bridge requires its own treatment policy rather than inheriting the foot assumption. An approved no-treatment choice is an authored departure unless source equivalence is established. Environment, age, conditioning, contact history and recovery remain explicit.

Every supported transfer has a source station/slot and observed occupant, retained carrier, destination vacancy, qualified transform, retention and safe-release evidence. S04 coordinates real custody occurrences, never teleportation. No robot grasps a fragile hinge or an optical pad. Mounted, moving, loaded or release-uncertain specimens cannot be carried away. A failed transfer retains actual supported custody and the relevant lease.

## Mechanics, imaging and synchronized acquisition
R06 independently qualifies mechanics after docking. The unloaded reference and contact zero are distinct, versioned records. Source speeds and strokes are planning references; they are not force limits, collision envelopes or qualified commands. Load-cell range/calibration, interlocks, safe stop/unload and force/stroke/speed limits remain external commissioning requirements.

R07 independently qualifies the camera, lens, focus, field of view, illumination, distortion map, exposure actually used and pixel-to-world uncertainty. Source Nikon/LED identifiers are metadata. The F4 lens name is not proof of the selected aperture. Reported 0.02 pixel/one-micrometre tracking accuracy is not a calibration certificate or guaranteed world-coordinate precision.

R08 holds exclusive tester and camera resources and joins current mechanics and imaging receipts to specimen, fixture, conditioning state and configuration epoch. Capture-ready and buffer-ready observations must precede machine motion. Camera-to-machine scale/offset, tolerance, endpoint convention and branch program are pinned. R09 retains frame IDs, original raw digests, phase labels, source clocks and one-to-one mechanical events. A composed synthetic guard checks current calibration and clock joins, rejects frames before machine start and checks arbitrary test-program values against arbitrary commissioned limits. No real acquisition or trusted device authentication is implemented.

Foot source references are 20 mm at 0.1 mm/s; bridge references are 40 mm at 0.2 mm/s. Both source cycles report 400 images at 1 frame/s. The two videos encode those images at 30 fps. Playback duration never becomes acquisition duration. Unreported dwell, acceleration and endpoint conventions mean that arithmetic derived from nominal stroke/rate cannot manufacture exact frame validity or elapsed duration. A new qualified run records its actual frame/event ledger, drops and partial data. It does not fabricate 400 acceptable images.

Loading and unloading remain distinct through acquisition, tracking and analysis. Friction, viscoelasticity and self-adhesion are evidence, not grounds to remove an inconvenient phase. An aborted loading-only cycle is archived as incomplete with its failure and all partial data; it does not become a complete cycle.

## Recovery, fixture changes and prospective repetition
R10 requires observed unloading and disarming, independent damage/residual/adhesion records and a new specimen condition state. Sending an unload command is insufficient. Unloading does not restore a virgin state. Reuse requires the frozen recovery criterion and an independent acceptance receipt; otherwise retain hold, quarantine or retirement.

R11 may follow either physical branch. Same-specimen fixture exchange records full prior history, advances configuration epoch, invalidates docking, mechanics, imaging and synchronization readiness and re-enters R05-R10 with a new run ID. A replacement specimen re-enters R02-R04 with its own preparation lineage. Reconditioning or treatment changes re-enter the applicable R03-R04 service steps. Old specimen receipts cannot authorize a replacement. The design dependency arrays are not an executable scheduler; the occurrence loop and failure edges are separate.

Before any outcome, R01 freezes independent specimen counts, cycles per condition, order, reset, uncertainty, analysis/model-order, exclusions, acceptance and stop rules. These values default to null outside arbitrary test fixtures. The source does not establish independent n or repeat allocation. Four hundred frames are not four hundred specimens. The repeat ledger retains distinct run IDs, per-run specimen-state advancement, all failed/blocked records and order deviations. Retry uses fresh attempt/run/raw identities, preserves the old failure/raw record and stays inside the original prospective policy. No repeat-until-good policy is permitted.

## Tracking and two different analyses
R12 binds original images, calibration, time mapping, correspondence, square IDs, coordinate conventions, masks, rejected tracks and uncertainty. No uninspected source code is treated as qualified. A future independently authored pipeline must be explicitly labeled, pinned and validated.

R13 keeps nearest conformal fitting and boundary-only inference separate. Fitting may use the declared eligible full point set. Boundary-only inference may use only the declared closed boundary data; interior target displacements may be evaluated afterward but cannot fit the inferred field, tune its order or optimize its rigid-body gauge. Boundary sampling, simple connectivity, point masks, conditioning and model order are prospective inputs. Coefficient counts must stay below point-count limits and follow a qualified order policy; the mathematical ceiling alone is not a complete model-selection rule.

The metric is normalized squared displacement residual, not a pointwise one-percent error or fraction of correctly placed squares. A zero displacement denominator is undefined and blocks a claimed normalized result. Translation/rotation/constant-offset conventions must be declared before outcomes. Alpha linear dilation and J area ratio are distinct variables. Positive det(F), local noncollapse and orientation checks are required; positive dilation inferred from det(C) alone cannot detect a reflection. Global injectivity/non-self-intersection needs a separate qualified method. Unverified global geometry may remain explicitly unverified; a positive local determinant is not a collision-free geometry certificate.

All five printed scientific conflict groups remain visible. C01 distinguishes the main Methods area-ratio wording from linear dilation definitions. C02 retains the different printed hinge power. C03 retains squared-variable, sign and deformation-gradient inconsistencies in stress theory. C04 retains polynomial constant-offset/registration ambiguity. C05 retains both the printed gradient modulus and a separately labeled scalar substitution discrepancy. No solver is silently corrected or certified. Physical fitting/inference specifically requires expert interpretation of C01/C04; energy/stress/linear-prediction claims remain blocked by the relevant additional conflicts. No physics solver is supplied.

The ideal rotating-square alpha interval is reference-dependent and does not define physical strength or machine stops. The literature greater-than-99-percent language does not become a universal physical threshold. Physical inference residuals during loading can differ substantially from best numerical curves, and both phases must remain visible.

## Numerical and theory scope
R14 preserves periodic-cell low-strain FEM, full-structure nonlinear FEM and hinge sweeps, hinge/coarse-grained energy and stress theory, perturbative prediction, and spring-network numerical boundary control. Printed numerical two-dimensional units remain intact. Simulation unit-cell dimensions and moduli do not replace physical specimen calibration. At least two elements through a feature and a named optimizer are not independent convergence evidence. Constrained nodes, solver/version settings, mesh convergence, topology/rest lengths and optimizer termination remain unverified archive dependencies.

No physical solid negative-control specimen, dipole/shear fixture, kagome apparatus or boundary actuator is invented. Remotely actuated struts are future work. The symbolic scene is not a laboratory station executing these branches. Literature numerical outputs are neither new observations nor actor targets.

## Failure and closeout
Any operation may enter a bounded failure closeout without successful R12-R15 analysis. Before receipt of any specimen, prephysical cancellation records no invented physical custody. During services/transfers/tests, supported hold retains the actual station and station-specific open lease; the robot stays outside a guarded uncertain state. After independently observed safe unload, disarm and release, the supported specimen can move to verified storage/quarantine with actual custody and disposition receipts. Unsupported removal or an unresolved lease cannot be reported as safe return.

R16 archives all nine branch dispositions, all failures, raw/derived provenance, actual specimen history and open gates. Archive completeness and scientific success are different predicates. The design default remains HOLD_QUALIFICATION. A supported unresolved hold is truthful accounting, not completed physical execution.

## Verification and asset pairing
The Python module is a finite offline metadata-guard model. Actor replay messages contain only event, operation and evidence IDs. Extra actor truth fields, altered canonical registries and mismatched operation/evidence order are rejected. Individual guard receipts are arbitrary evaluator fixtures, not authenticated real sensors. The finite replay exercises operation templates and composed acquisition joins; it is not a scheduler, a complete multicycle run or production security isolation.

The paired original scene uses A01-A12, ASSET.Axx roots and ANCHOR.Rxx.primary/control with concrete object selectors. Additional semantic anchors use the source requirements. World-space poses are nominal and physically unqualified. The task pins final geometry and semantic bytes only after asset review and freeze. File/ZIP validity does not validate contacts, grasps, reachability, collisions, camera optics, mechanics or hardware safety.
