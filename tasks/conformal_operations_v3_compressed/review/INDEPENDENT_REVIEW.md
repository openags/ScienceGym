## Contract findings and corrections

Preparation precedes acquisition and preserves material-lot, print-job, specimen, pad-layout, fixture, treatment, carrier and conditioning-state lineage. Service requests cannot impersonate inspected completion. Unknown powder is blocked. Replacement specimens and changed treatment re-enter applicable preparation; unloading cannot erase prior history or restore a virgin specimen.

Independent review found and verified six correction groups:

1. Acquisition now composes current mechanics and camera calibration, configuration epoch, clock mapping, branch program, commissioned program envelope and machine start. Stale or retimed records fail. Boolean epochs cannot impersonate integer epochs
2. Repeat runs require nonempty distinct IDs and advancing same-specimen condition states. Frames cannot replace independent specimens, and failed/blocked runs remain in the frozen allocation
3. Supported holds retain actual station-specific custody and leases. Cancellation before specimen receipt invents no custody. Failure closeout is available from any operation without successful scientific analysis
4. ZIP archive comments, member comments and extra fields cannot carry unlisted payloads. JSON-decoded private paths are rejected, including escaped Windows and Unicode forms
5. A UTF-8 BOM cannot conceal a media signature under an allowed text filename
6. Asset pairing checks actual GLB hierarchy, composed transforms, mesh ownership and anchor-target metadata rather than relying only on mutually consistent JSON tables

Both loading and unloading survive acquisition and tracking. An aborted partial cycle remains incomplete. Nearest conformal fitting and boundary-only inference remain distinct: interior target displacements and post-hoc gauge optimization cannot leak into boundary inference. Linear dilation and area ratio remain separate. Positive det(F) and local noncollapse do not certify global injectivity; a global geometry claim requires a separate qualified receipt. Undefined residual normalization is rejected. No favorable source result or ideal mechanism interval becomes a universal experimental target or safety limit. All five scientific conflicts remain visible without a silently corrected solver.
