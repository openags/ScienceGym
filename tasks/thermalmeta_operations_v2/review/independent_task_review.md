## Corrected findings

- T-A01 (high): Cross-specimen object-ID reuse. Fresh receipt IDs allowed a different specimen to reuse a previous preparation object chain. Episode-wide preparation object IDs are now checked before mutation.
- T-A02 (medium): Malformed material-lot collection. A string passed the material_lot_ids iterable check. A nonempty list of nonempty strings is now required.
- T-A03 (high): Duplicate independent-repeat identity. Two slots could call the same specimen and condition two independent repeats. All nontechnical specimen/condition pairs must now be unique; technical repeats retain separate slots.
- T-A04 (high): Observation source and numerical-payload leakage. Transient and stability tokens accepted source origins, and transient/steady tokens accepted numerical array extras. All three observation stages now require explicit synthetic_metadata_only provenance and restrict keys to their metadata schemas.
- T-A05 (medium): Camera and dataset parent consistency. A transient token could name an unrelated camera configuration. P12 matches the current calibration configuration and is retained; P14 also matches its dataset ID.
- T-A06 (high): Out-of-order powered observation bypassed abort route. The failed ordering assertion left P11 active without a failure record. Ordering checks now enter the guarded failure path, record the failed attempt and route to P15 while custody stays service-owned.
- T-A07 (medium): Nonportable archive paths. Drive-qualified and backslash traversal member names were accepted. Backslash, colon and control characters are rejected before relative-path checks.

## Source and release boundary

Ten upstream provenance hashes match the accepted review packet. The six reused factual/hold/control registers and the failure catalog are byte-identical to their upstream authored counterparts. This audit did not acquire publisher media or rerun source-access checks. All prose in this review is newly authored.

The engine is an offline synthetic bookkeeping demonstration. It does not authenticate service certificates, validate real safe states, implement all future service fields, operate hardware, solve physics or produce experimental observations. Acceptance can contribute at most one paired original paper-level design unit. Validated runnable scientific tasks, experiments and numerical results remain zero.

Final release still requires the accepted scene, identical binding bytes, exact export allowlist, final content manifests, reciprocal pair seal and independent ZIP inspection. The final archive audit must be detached from the sealed payload.
