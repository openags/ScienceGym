# Task design and evidence boundary

## Purpose
Train or inspect safe evidence coordination across one complete experimental paper without providing hazardous operating steps. The actor manages identities, dependencies, qualification holds, orientation metadata, evidence registration and custody. Every external service is a receipt interface; nothing in this package powers equipment or commands real motion.

## Scientific reference versus authored design
The reviewed source reports three thermal devices (cloak, 45-degree rotator, 1.8-target concentrator) in X/Y boundary conditions. The six cells do not establish six independent specimens. Independent fabrication and technical-repeat counts are undisclosed. Reported constituent/material values and the nominal 120 × 120 × 4.5 mm envelope remain reference annotations, not lot certificates or physical-fit qualification.

The study organization, controls, identity scheme, task stages, scene, custody state machine and tests are authored additions. Qualified physical controls, calibration bracketing, repeated reseating and independent-specimen planning are requirements for a future approved study; none is claimed to have been reported or performed here.

## Route
P01–P03 lock source, plan and design. P04–P07 separately bind material/tooling, lattice, filled intermediate and final encapsulated specimen receipts. P08 accepts only the matching safely released specimen. P09 qualifies calibration and planned controls. P10 stages a verified orientation only in an already qualified dry/ambient unpowered fixture. P11 transfers custody to the thermal service. P12 registers transient metadata; P13 requires actual qualified stability evidence; P14 registers a calibrated steady result. P15 always owns safe release, including after failure or stop. P16 traverses approved orientations, repeats and specimens without collapsing identities. P17 links real matched controls. P18 assesses qualified evidence under an explicit metric contract. P19 registers all broader analytical/numerical branches as unexecuted. P20 reconciles data, failures, claims and material custody.

All powered-stage failures route to P15. If release is missing or uncertain, service custody persists. Administrative incomplete closure records the open obligation but cannot transfer material or become success. A retry never erases its failed predecessor. A new family or specimen must acquire its own design/lattice/intermediate/final chain before entry at P08.

## Source gates
The printed squared-error metric ratios disagree with the main-text norm language (C01). The mapping equation index is inconsistent (C02). The coarsest cloak feature labels disagree (C03). Coordinate scale and absolute flux remain unreconciled (C05). These are explicit gates, not corrected silently. Other holds preserve hybrid iterative/numerical scope, label/phase inconsistencies, nonmonotonic feature cases, adiabatic-interpretation differences and unspecified solver handling.

The 45-minute source interval does not prove stability. Controller reference is not a measured specimen boundary. Rotator R1/R2 profiles are transverse to the applied gradient; cloak K1/K2 and concentrator C1/C2 profiles follow it. A near-zero denominator holds the metric. IR temperature does not directly establish absolute heat flux. No source numerical percentage is a physical pass/fail criterion.

## Test interpretation
The standard-library engine accepts and rejects synthetic identity and Boolean receipt tokens. It does not authenticate signatures or establish a physical safe state. Its tests exercise wrong-family and stale-parent lineage, repeated IDs, wrong orientation, calibration, stability, custody/abort transitions, missing controls, metadata leakage and incomplete closeout. Passing tests is only evidence that these finite authored bookkeeping cases behaved as specified.

## Count
This package and its accepted original scene form at most one original paper-level design unit. Orientations, stages, numerical variants, tests and repeated specimens add zero paper units. Validated runnable scientific tasks, new experiments and numerical results remain zero.
