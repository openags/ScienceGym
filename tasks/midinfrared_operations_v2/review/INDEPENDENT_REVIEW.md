# Independent mid-infrared task review

## Verdict

Accepted for release as an original whole-paper task design with finite synthetic contracts and nominal scene bindings. There are no remaining blocking design findings in the reviewed content. The validated runnable whole-paper task count remains **zero**.

This review does not establish hardware operation, robot execution, optical simulation, physical safety, source-data reanalysis or scientific reproduction.

This reviewer compared the authored task to that packet rather than repeating its original page, figure or movie inspection. The task covers 22 operations, eight branches, five stations, thirteen asset families and twelve control packages. Preparation retains material lot, drawing, fabrication completion, specimen, carrier and independent-reference lineage. Protected service handoffs, copper return, silicon loading, planned reloads, final custody and every branch disposition remain explicit.

Source facts, authored robot requirements, nominal geometry and synthetic records are separated. Approximately 10 Hz remains an analog 16x16 reference, with 2.5 Hz for analog 32x32. It never becomes a photon-sparse success threshold. B01 requires spatial diagnostic sensing and distinguishes complete pump-series evidence from representative SFG evidence. B05 detector/exposure remains unresolved in the source representation; any synthetic resolution requires an explicit, current-epoch, independently labeled branch policy. Random-mask counting and the silicon grid also remain unresolved.

## Findings resolved during independent review

1. B05 could inherit generic readiness without detector/exposure resolution. It now requires a bound independent branch policy and rejects spatial-diagnostic mode
2. An occupied supported hold could omit its open lease. The lease must now be retained
3. The exporter missed additional private absolute-path families and local file locators. These are now rejected
4. Recognized binary/media signatures could hide under allowed text filenames. The text filter now rejects them
5. Composed acquisition checked readiness only at a supplied timestamp. It now checks the complete duration of each raw exposure, with the authored timestamp convention stated
6. B07 could accept a Hadamard ledger. The composed guard now enforces grounded encoding restrictions for B02/B03/B04 and B07

Independent tests confirm current-epoch readiness, sample/run/lease binding, mask-controller-detector bijection, adjacent complementary records, explicit normalization, paired-analysis provenance, prospective repeats, pseudoreplication rejection, fresh retry identities, retained failures and truthful closeout. Canonical replay never upgrades a source outcome or held state into scientific success.
