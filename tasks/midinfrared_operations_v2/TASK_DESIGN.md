# Mid-infrared whole-paper task design

## Complete route, bounded claims
The task represents all eight experimental branches from the accepted source review: spatial mapping, static analog letters, dynamic analog imaging, full photon-sparse copper imaging, encoding comparison, fixed-flux integration comparison, random compressed/denoised imaging and silicon transmission. Source theory and numerical predictions are explicitly unexecuted dependencies. Future biomedical, general-scene, depth and terahertz applications do not become tasks. Full source pages and eleven figures were reviewed upstream; movies were only visually sampled after complete decoding.

Source facts, authored interfaces, nominal geometry and synthetic test data remain different provenance classes. The source values in branches.json and source_parameters.json are references for planning, not commands or automatic success thresholds. Main/SI raw data and modified reconstruction code were not obtained. Nothing here establishes reproduction, hardware safety, optical accuracy or robot reachability.

## Preparation and custody
Copper E/C/N/U masks and etched silicon-star samples retain distinct material-lot, drawing, fabrication-job, specimen, carrier and independent-reference identities. Preparation is represented as specification, protected stock handoff, qualified service completion, safe release and independent inspection. A request cannot stand in for fabrication completion. The robot handles cassette perimeter surfaces only; fabrication and etching internals are excluded. Silicon source thickness is a material fact, not full carrier geometry. A replacement gets new sample and series identities.

Every movement binds a source station/slot, observed occupant, destination vacancy, sample, retained carrier, transform revision, gripper retention and current safe-release evidence. Copper loading, copper return, silicon loading, repeat reloads and final return are explicit occurrences. Co-location does not erase custody. Mounted, acquiring or unsafe-to-release carriers cannot be transported.

## Closed optical services and cross-device handoff
S03 is one exclusive leased station for acquisition, motion or configuration change. External qualified services own enclosed optical readiness, flux calibration, alignment, detector protection and guarded motion. The task contains no laser controls, exposed alignment, power optimization or interlock bypass. A controller request is never proof of a safe state. Robot access requires an independently observed current safe-access receipt. An uncertain station leaves the robot outside the enclosure.

Each configuration epoch binds detector mode, source/filter/calibration identities, controller revision and allowed branches. A detector change occurs only while idle and safe, advances the epoch and invalidates stale readiness. Every subsequent acquisition checks current branch compatibility, not merely whether a photon readiness receipt once existed. B05 has unresolved source detector/exposure configuration and cannot silently inherit the preceding photon mode. B06, B07 and B08 require fresh photon-compatible readiness after any intervening configuration change.

The raw ledger enforces agreement among expected DMD display IDs, controller counter events and detector records. Physical displays and signed coefficients are different entities. Full Hadamard coefficients have consecutive complementary displays, both raw observations and a pinned matrix normalization. Missing, duplicate, reordered, overloaded or incompatible records invalidate the affected set; no interpolation or invented counts close it. Random compression has unresolved physical-display convention and must explicitly resolve it before acquisition.

## Spatial mapping and eight branches
B01 requires spatial diagnostic pump/SFG images from a separately qualified sensor and registration service; the single bucket detector is insufficient. Object-removed state and an empty optical dock are explicit. All-on, uncorrected pump, corrected pump and representative SFG evidence remain separate. Movie 1 establishes only the pump-series visualization, not full-set SFG fidelity. A supplied correction policy governs valid pixels and near-zero exclusion. No threshold is inferred from an attractive image.

B02 preserves all four copper letters and the two source grids. B03 preserves analog-only dynamic modes; 16x16 approximately 10 Hz and 32x32 approximately 2.5 Hz are source references including reconstruction. Movie playback speed and container frame rate are distinct. B04 retains the five paired flux/accumulation conditions at both grids. B05 retains three encodings at three grids and cannot equate same illumination power with same total dose. B06 retains five exposures at three grids at fixed incident mean flux. B07 retains baseline and denoised source conditions separately; any newly claimed paired comparison must use exactly the same raw measurements. B08 retains a separate silicon sample, blank/reference and three flux/exposure conditions, with missing grid policy left unresolved rather than inferred from copper.

Pixel grid and DMD pitch are not spatial resolution. Incident mean photons per pulse is not total image dose or detected count. Full-Hadamard minimum flux and compressed/denoised minimum flux are separate reported regimes. None is a guaranteed evaluator outcome.

## Analysis, controls and repetition
Immutable raw files, pattern order, calibration, correction, software, weights, parameters, independent target reference and uncertainty policy are parents of each result. Source figures are not target truth; unavailable source code is not silently replaced. An explicitly approved independent implementation would be labeled as authored and separately validated. A clean denoised image does not by itself validate structure; baseline, data-space residual, clipping and independent truth remain necessary.

R01 freezes specimen/run/reload/day counts, order, budget, metrics, uncertainty and stopping policy before any outcome. R06 confirms that freeze. R20 expands only that existing plan and re-enters acquisition with fresh run IDs and current epochs. It cannot plan repeats from first-run results or keep trying until an appealing image appears. Masks, laser pulses, complementary pairs and frames are nested acquisition units, not independent experimental n. Missing repeat counts and numeric limits remain null and block physical execution.

## Failure and closeout
Every operation can record a non-success disposition and enter qualified recovery without scientific success. Failed raw records and prior-failure links are never erased. A retry receives fresh attempt/run/raw identities and preserves the same prospective policy. If actual independent safe-unload evidence exists, the task closes the correct dock lease, returns each protected sample to verified storage/quarantine, inspects it and reconciles all resources. If safety or release is uncertain, the valid accounting state is supported unresolved hold with actual custody and open leases retained. That is not safe unloading or completed execution.

All eight branch statuses, sample custody, accepted/failed/blocked records, services and leases must be present in the archive. An analysis hold cannot hide a missing experimental branch. Archive completeness and scientific success are different predicates.

## Verification and asset boundary
The Python code implements strict symbolic guards and evaluator-pinned finite synthetic replay. Actor messages contain only event, operation and evidence identifiers. Additional sensor/success fields and altered fixture registries are rejected. This does not authenticate real sensors or implement production actor/evaluator isolation. Synthetic arbitrary values are not measurements or source simulation.

The paired scene uses A01-A13, ASSET.Axx roots and ANCHOR.Rxx.primary/control anchors with concrete object selectors. Bindings are pinned after the final asset freeze. Original nominal world-space geometry supplies semantic correspondence only. It cannot close physical dimension, friction, grip, optical, fixture, calibration, safety or reach gaps.
