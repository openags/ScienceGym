- Preparation is now physical robot work: sandwich and PDMS-blanket preparation, flat-film making, protective coating, custom-particle preparation, and collision-guide fabrication have object/tool identities, manipulation actions, device boundaries, preconditions, and observable evidence
- Prepared specimens are no longer an unexplained substitute for those manufacturing responsibilities; missing recipes and geometry remain honest local gates
- Collision arms distinguish Stage1 targets from shape-adapted targets, with explicit cure-state constraints and a separate retained-bead baseline before impact
- Postcollision images have a causal predecessor event; preparation, custom-particle manufacturing, optical acquisition, and analysis dependencies are explicit
- Reuse records precede reset, and reset at cycle n precedes loading cycle n+1; the historical meaning of the reported reuse count stays qualified
- Transfer and analysis station references resolve. Transport remains a real custody/support obligation, distinct from a device process or a data handoff
- Destructive removal produces separately identified remnants and beads; it cannot silently preserve an intact array identity

## Export assessment and remaining limits

The final directory contains authored text/JSON/Python and validation logs. Temporary publisher PDFs, screenshots, extracted text, movie descriptions and figure renderings have been removed. The source's CC BY-NC-ND notice is retained and no repository license is changed. This is an asset-boundary review, not a legal license opinion.

Historical movies/data and main-figure pixels remain uninspected as disclosed. Exact geometry, some material recipes, control allocation and device/acquisition methods require qualified inputs. No runtime actor-projection loader is implemented; mounting the entire review directory to an actor would violate the stated boundary. These limits do not prevent this clearly gated design draft from representing robot participation across the reported practical program.
