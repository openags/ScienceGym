# Independent source and contract review checklist

Prepared from the existing lawful source audit and the conversion package's provenance, conflicts and unknown-input ledgers. This is a review plan, not an execution protocol. Source acquisition, physical work and physical simulation are excluded.

## Required scientific coverage

- Distinct lineage for quantitative 5×5 macro Taiji positive/negative specimens and the illustrative 4×4 manually loaded video demonstrator
- Distinct micro Taiji 5×6 fabrication, 4×4 compression and 5×5 four-motor actuation families
- Separate planetary steel 3×3 compression and 2×2 tension; macro polymer 6×6 compression/actuation and 2×6 tension; micro polymer 3×4 compression/actuation and 2×3 tension
- Separate steel-frame control, finite 3×3 shear lattice, 4×4-metacell ultrasoft lattice, rubber-frame control and 3×3 impact polymer specimen
- Preserve metallic copper/brass uncertainty, polymer 2.5-GPa versus impact 2.0-GPa lineage, printed micro material declaration, and rubber/aluminum branch identity
- Preserve unresolved micro sun radius, planet radius and planetary/transmission tooth-module row identity; no inferred repair of source geometry
- Preserve measured range versus theoretical-curve ratio in steel planetary compression; source outcomes are contextual, not universal pass thresholds
- Preserve measured damping points alongside numerical damping, with tooth-friction and shaft-friction distinctions
- Distinguish finite-structure physical shear stiffness from periodic homogenized numerical modulus
- Keep skin/application/catalog/literature/body-weight demonstrations illustrative; sampled keyframes do not establish full-motion or audio review

## Physical operation coverage

Every physical branch must declare qualified inputs and observable outputs for the applicable sequence below. Gate placeholders may establish design completeness but cannot establish execution readiness.

1. Finite condition/specimen/repeat allocation, identity and lot acceptance
2. Certified geometry/material/process cards and fabrication or qualified incoming-part acceptance
3. Support removal or boxed/baseplate release when applicable, preserving tooth clearance and phase
4. Tooth, shaft, frame and transmission-layer assembly; meshing and free-spin inspection
5. Supported grasp, transport route, orientation and station-frame registration, with handover evidence
6. Fixture installation and zeroing: parallel compression faces, tension-tail clamps, finite-shear guide/rollers/pins, or opposed right-angle grooves as appropriate
7. Phase/polarity/front-back registration and pre-load/post-load confirmation; calibrated motor homing and synchronization where applicable
8. Sensor calibration, traceable zeros, acquisition arming, synchronized raw force/displacement/image records and explicit normalization geometry
9. Qualified finite load/cycle/rate/force/strain envelope, stop policy, first-cycle exclusion and retained late-cycle data
10. Abort recovery, controlled unload, de-energization, damage/reuse classification, safe removal, waste routing/storage and terminal specimen allocation
11. Impact-specific enclosed service, interlock/exclusion/load-capacity proof, arming/release separation, acquisition readiness, safe reset and no general-manipulator release substitute

## Contract and adversarial checks

- Deny missing, empty, null, non-finite, wrong-type, negative and unknown branch/operation inputs as applicable
- Deny bool-as-number values, strings coerced to booleans, zero/empty allocation and duplicated identities/records where uniqueness matters
- Reject evidence records for another specimen, condition, operation, material lot, phase, station, operator/service or run
- Enforce actual dependency order and monotonic state; recording a final receipt cannot stand in for unrecorded physical predecessors
- Enforce scope-specific gate closure; a material certificate does not close geometric contradictions and an analysis-only choice does not authorize fabrication
- Ensure impact release fails closed without each independent required safety condition, including after an abort, stale receipt or reset
- Separate actor-visible tasks and observations from evaluator-only answers, reference outcomes and protected evidence; unknown metadata must not bypass the boundary
- Require nonempty post-initial cycles, fit-window identities and actual uncertainty semantics; do not label fit-window variability as specimen standard deviation
- Refuse measured/model/illustrative relabeling or joining across incompatible specimen families and materials
- Check all emitted artifacts, source locators, conflict references, unknown-gate references, branch references and file paths for referential integrity
- Mutation tests must verify rejected behavior, not merely the existence of labels or a changed hash
- Author tests and independently authored checks must be run against the final reviewed snapshot; record commands, counts, failures and review limits

## Review acceptance boundary

A successful local review can establish a source-grounded, internally consistent, fail-closed task design. It cannot certify fabrication feasibility, station safety, real robot execution, numerical reproduction, source corrections or scientific replication. Execution remains blocked until each qualified gate is actually resolved for a concrete campaign.
