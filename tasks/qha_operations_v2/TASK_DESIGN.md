# Graphene quantum Hall array: whole-paper task design

This is one original service-mediated task-design package, not a runnable robot, scientific reproduction, physical simulation or operating manual. Physical execution is disabled and all qualification is held. The finite tests validate metadata rules only.

## Source and complete route

He and colleagues, *Accurate graphene quantum Hall arrays for the new International System of Units*, Nature Communications 13, 6933 (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-34680-0, provides the source. The accepted review read all nine main pages and four SI pages, all five main and four SI figures, the full nine-row main table and four numbered equations. Raw data and original code/CAD are unavailable here; the optional peer-review file was not read. Crossmark was inaccessible and the no-matching-notice result is only a bounded check. Exact accepted annotations are retained with hashes.

The 15 operations R00–R14 preserve the full preparation-to-closeout route. R00 approves scope, source decisions and qualification. R01 receives supplier, wafer/die and specialist process release. R02 registers protected carrier, condition and accepted custody. R03 records specialist handover, mount, calibration, terminal map and exclusive resource leases. R04 covers ordinary Hall-bar and subarray characterization. R05 is direct Array1/Array2 precision comparison. R06 compares the Hall bar to the 100-ohm reference. R07 keeps both subarray-to-100-ohm edges separate. R08 covers direct Hall-bar/Array1 checks in both field directions. R09 represents intentionally nonquantizing controls. R10 separately represents short high-bias screening and long records. R11 represents the distinct 12.9-kiloohm external check. R12 preserves immutable evidence and only qualified analysis. R13 reviews directed comparison paths, covariance and limits. R14 closes every actually started service job and returns accepted custody, including partial/failed branches.

All hazardous fabrication, precision electrical, cryogenic, strong-field, vacuum/pressure and bath operations remain closed specialist services. Preparation is represented by a full release contract, never omitted and never replaced by a recipe. The package cannot send requests to actual services. Only original illustrative static asset bindings and metadata-only tests are implemented.

## Identity and uncertainty

Array1 and Array2 are two electrical regions of one chip, each with 118 parallel Hall elements and nominal R_K/236, about 109 ohm. The series combination contains 236 elements and has nominal R_K/118, about 219 ohm. The on-chip Hall bar is a third region of the same physical chip. It is not an external resistor or an independent fabricated specimen. The 100-ohm oil-bath reference and 12.9-kiloohm air-bath reference retain distinct actual identities, calibration, bath and per-device current/power records.

All 29 source facts, 18 ambiguities and 14 reported outcomes stay unchanged. Reported means, currents, temperatures, fields, uncertainties and equipment specifications are historical context only. They are never runtime defaults, safe limits, success thresholds or generated observations. No raw measurement series exists in this package. The source graph has five direct edge types, four nodes, three simple loops and two independent graph cycles. There is no reported direct Hall-bar/Array2 edge. Derived paths reuse measurements and cannot be counted as independent acquisitions.

## Evidence and independent repeats

Every future accepted bundle must preserve physical chip and region, supplier/process, carrier/mount/custodian, exclusive device resource IDs, reference/test roles, ratio orientation, configuration/calibration/method revisions, actual conditions, separate current/power records, timestamps, polarity cycles, per-reading uncertainties, raw hashes, exclusions, failures and covariance links. Configuration or reference changes invalidate affected calibration chains. Calibration must be valid throughout the acquisition interval.

Source CCC reading counts do not become independent chip repeats. Fabrication lot, chip, cooldown, remount and independent provider/lab are different experimental units. Prospective numbers, ordering/randomization, drift bracketing, exclusions and targets remain null pending qualification. Failed and held units remain in the lineage rather than silently disappearing.

## Explicit analysis holds

Printed Eq 3 has unresolved input-representation/units semantics. Eq 4 conflicts with surrounding bin-size wording and has an unresolved percent convention. The SI loop expansion repeats the wrong edge. These are explicit blockers to affected numerical analysis; no numerical Allan or loop implementation is included. The off-plateau direct/indirect discrepancy, high-bias table/plot sign, unnamed SI4 subarray and 2-versus-2.1 K labels also remain visible. No equation is silently repaired, no source claim is promoted to verified closure, and no common-mode result uniquely identifies a microscopic cause.

## Failure and closeout

A scientific disagreement is a valid observation. It does not request automatic higher current, longer averaging or deletion of inconvenient readings. Lost communications preserve the last accepted support, custodian and leases. A job timeout, stop request or finished analysis is not safe-state proof.

R14 is not gated on R12/R13 success. It joins every actually started job. Electrical accessibility, accessible magnetic-field state, thermal and pressure/vacuum accessibility, stopped/supported mechanisms and receiving support/identity/occupancy require independently observed qualified receipts. Until accepted, custody and leases remain with the provider. Final per-branch complete, partial, failed, held and unattempted outcomes remain distinct; provider-held quarantine is not falsely relabeled returned custody.

## Qualification and reuse

The original static scene uses AS01–AS11 with evidence-only anchors. Its geometry, scale, grasp, collision and transfer envelope are illustrative and unqualified. Closed doors, color indicators and green labels do not certify safety. Source photographs, figures, PDFs, page renders, full extracted text, raw data, CAD and code are excluded from export.
