# First complete route: matched spin-coated module comparison

DOI: [10.1038/s41586-024-07228-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07228-z)

## Scope and actor-facing task

Create two separately traceable, eight-subcell module proxies, one with the paper's MACl control formulation and one with its MACl/[Bcmim]Cl target formulation. Obtain masked forward/reverse electrical records and a separate stabilized-output record for each. Encapsulate each proxy, assign it to the requested room-temperature or heated operational-ageing condition, preserve the ageing history, and finish with archived samples, linked records, contained waste and reset workstations.

The actor starts with unused FTO proxy plates, sealed symbolic raw-material lots, empty labelled carriers, unopened layer-material cartridges, cover-glass proxies, contact and sealant tokens, empty stopped service stations and a condition card. No finished substrate, perovskite film, module, acquisition or future observation exists. The actor receives the goal, object labels, formulation/measurement cards and observable device feedback. It does not receive the reference route below, hidden defects, completion flags, or desired scientific results.

This is an authored mobile-robot task design. All chemical, hot, vacuum, beam, laser, sputtering, evaporation, UV and electrical processing is an inert closed-service state machine. The robot transports objects, loads carriers, registers jobs, seats masks and reversible connectors, observes public status, retrieves released objects and saves mock acquisitions. No real chemical processing, hardware control, scientific reproduction or physics simulation is authorized or claimed.

## Evaluator-only reference route

1. Inspect inventory and station availability. Read the two formulation IDs; reserve distinct substrate, lot, carrier and job IDs. Reject mismatched or exhausted symbolic portions before transfer.
2. Retrieve and support each unused FTO proxy. Register its face, orientation and material lot; put it in a dedicated carrier. Module substrate size is reported as 6.5 × 7.0 cm; scene geometry is an unimplemented binding requirement.
3. Take each carrier to the closed scribing service. Load and seat it, close the guard, register a P1 job, wait for completed plus safe-release, then unload. The source places P1 before the functional stack; a scored P1 record is required before any layer deposition.
4. Move the P1 plate to the closed cleaning service. Load the plate and assigned acetone, isopropanol and water proxy tokens in the source order; obtain the three ultrasonic-cleaning state transitions, collect the released plate and retain each spent-token record. The source reports 15 min but does not unambiguously distinguish total from per-solvent time; the mock service duration is not a new scientific claim.
5. Register a Ti-stock preparation token and its cold-storage history; select the Ti compact-layer job. The closed service consumes the correct Ti-stock/diluent tokens and plate reservation, applies the reported bath stage, returns a cooled plate for the rinse service, records the three ethanol/water rinse cycles and then the Ti-layer anneal. Each transfer requires a new carrier receipt and safe release.
6. Register the Sn modification job on that Ti-layer plate. The source's Sn-stock description and its dilution parenthesis conflict. Preserve both source claims in metadata; do not silently choose a real chemical recipe. This mock-only episode uses a visibly labelled unresolved-source service card and records that exception. A source-reproduction interpretation would stop here pending clarification.
7. Retrieve the modified plate only after safe release, complete its separately recorded rinse cycles and anneal service, and inspect the identity and completed-layer history.
8. Prepare the PMMA:PCBM proxy solution from its three assigned symbolic inputs through a closed preparation service. Transport the plate and solution cartridge to coating, load and guard, run the reported spin-layer job, retrieve at safe release, then hand off to the separate anneal service. Mixing, coating and heating are distinct state transitions.
9. Allocate separate control and target precursor lots. For each, register CsCl, MAI, FAI, PbI2, DMF/DMSO and MACl symbolic portions; only the target also consumes [Bcmim]Cl. Seal and register the preparation/stirring service. Never produce the control by deleting a target ingredient after mixing.
10. Move the correct prepared precursor cartridge and its paired layered plate to the closed spin service. Register the source-reported multistage spin program, load/guard/start, wait, retrieve and record a wet-film state. Returning the wet film to inventory does not make it pristine.
11. Transfer that wet film to the rapid-vacuum-drying proxy. Seat the carrier, close/acknowledge the job and wait for completion and release. Preserve the source's device-film drying route separately from the longer initial-film drying used for HIM-SIMS; do not substitute one for the other.
12. Transfer the dried film through the two sequential anneal-service jobs. Cooling/release and explicit handoff separate the stages; thermal history accumulates and cannot be erased by a retry.
13. Prepare/register PEAI solution token, load the annealed film in coating, complete the PEAI coating job and retrieve the released layer stack. Prepare/register the doped Spiro-OMeTAD token from its reported constituents, then complete the separate hole-transport coating job.
14. Take the stack to the closed scribing service and complete P2. A P2-complete, electrode-free stack is the only valid input to the next service.
15. Perform three ordered closed deposition handoffs: MoO3, ITO, then Au. Verify the required input-layer state, load and guard, register the correct layer job, wait for complete plus release, unload and retain the layer receipt at every handoff. Deposition is irreversible; failed stacks are quarantined, not stripped by renaming.
16. Return the electrode-complete stack to scribing for P3. Retrieve it as an eight-subcell module proxy only after the P3 receipt and identity check. An authored inspection may detect an open/short connection; it cannot infer scientific performance.
17. Bring the module, mask and reference-cell proxy to the electrical station. Seat and clamp the module, place the 27.22 cm² aperture mask, connect the reversible leads, register the reference check and verify the publicly visible calibration status. The source's device-intensity unit is inconsistent; the mock card preserves that issue rather than relabelling it silently.
18. Acquire a forward scan, save its raw record and direction; acquire a separate reverse scan and save it under the same device ID with a new run ID. The source module range/step/delay are condition metadata. A mock number appears only after an acquisition event; no paper PCE is treated as a measured robot result.
19. Run a separate maximum-power-point acquisition with elapsed-time records. Scan-derived performance, mean stabilized output and final stabilized output remain distinct. Stop output, verify zero/safe state, support the module, disconnect leads, remove the mask, release the clamp and unload. Repeating a scan does not create another module replicate.
20. Move the module to packaging. Record closed-service edge cleaning, register FTO/Au edge contacts, lay the indium contact proxy, place the separate cover-glass proxy, add the edge-seal token and transfer the assembly into the guarded UV-curing service. Retrieve only after its completed/released receipt. Cured encapsulation is an irreversible child state, not a reversible lid.
21. Assign each encapsulated module to one predeclared operational-ageing condition. Pair control and target within that condition; room-temperature and heated conditions require independent matched module pairs. Mount, connect and place the irradiance reference beside each module; confirm the appropriate public condition feedback before starting MPP tracking.
22. Accumulate mock elapsed-time and acquisition events under the same device ID up to the requested observation window. Explicit time compression is allowed but recorded; it is not a real 1,000 h test. On drift or dropout, preserve invalid intervals, stop safely, correct only reversible mounting/connection issues and restart as a new attempt. Irreversible degradation remains in the object's history.
23. Stop the service, await trustworthy safe release, disconnect while supporting the object and retrieve. Archive each module separately with its full lineage and condition history. Store missing/calibration-invalid outputs as unknown rather than fabricating values.
24. Return masks, carriers and tools; close and segregate all spent material tokens; clear only empty released stations. Submit the object-location manifest, source-issue exceptions, acquisition records, failures/recoveries and cleanup receipts.

## What is sourced and what is authored

Fabrication order, layer identities, eight-subcell structure, P1/P2/P3 placement, masks, measurement types and encapsulation route are reported in main PDF pp. 8–10; Fig. 1 is on p. 2. Operational-ageing conditions are in Methods p. 9 and Supplementary Fig. S33 (SI PDF p. 43). External certification and its three consecutive forward/reverse tests and 300 s tracking belong to Fig. 1 and SI S5–S6 (PDF pp. 12–13); this route does not impersonate certification.

Carriers, receipts, guards, portable token portions, task ordering between independent families, the paired minimum inventory, mock time compression, checkpoint mechanics and recoveries are authored benchmark connections. The source does not report one end-to-end chronological robot workflow, sample allocation, every handling step, complete coating geometries or every replicate count. Those gaps remain explicit.

## Relation to the complete package
