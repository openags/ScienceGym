# Independent review: transistor task design

## Result and scope

No unresolved blocker remains in the reviewed original design and offline synthetic contract. All identified implementation and lifecycle findings were fixed and independently retested. Release approval still requires an independent check of the final frozen export manifest after this review is integrated.

This review covers source-grounded task design and bookkeeping only. It does not approve semiconductor fabrication, live electrical sourcing, robotics, physical simulation, numerical reproduction, scientific replication or production safety use. The mock context is a caller-owned synthetic fixture, not an authenticated authority.
