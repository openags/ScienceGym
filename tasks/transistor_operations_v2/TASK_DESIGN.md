# Whole-paper transistor operations design

## Scope and truth boundary

This original English ScienceGym conversion accounts for the nine-page article and all 53 pages of its written supplement: six main figures, 33 supplementary figures, six tables and eight sections. It contains 24 bounded branch designs covering 17 audited route groups. Read branches.json, control_packages.json and coverage_matrix.json together. The design has no robot adapter, real actuation, physics engine or implemented scientific data analysis.

Fabrication, chemical preparation, deposition, lithography, cross-section preparation, ultrasonic bonding, electrical sourcing and thermal control are closed qualified services. The actor handles approved retained carriers, fixture interfaces, loading, readout and custody through externally qualified geometry. Missing recipes and mechanical settings are not supplied by inference. Qualification cards and independent observations are authored bridges, not claims that the authors used robotic equipment.

## End-to-end physical routes

1. PREP and STACK retain certified Si/SiO2 input, the reported cleaning sequence and monolithic layer order. The 72-layer inventory is two substrate layers, 60 active layers, nine interstack buffers and one final cap. There is no freestanding-film transfer. The 25/50 nm buffer discrepancy remains unresolved. Parylene mass calibration and full sputter/lithography recipes are still missing.
2. OVERLAP and HIGHK keep unsuccessful architecture/dielectric controls distinct from the final parylene device. Only HfO2 has a source-stated 250 C ALD condition. No successful NMP repair, CMP or anneal route is invented.
3. CROSSBAR and GATE_LEAKAGE distinguish 5-by-5 micrometre crossbars from the gate-only multilayer test. The latter has BG1/TG1/BG2/TG2/BG3 and no source/drain contacts. Four regions, three gate thicknesses and a source study criterion remain separate from production safety limits.
4. STRUCTURAL and SURFACE cover destructive cross-section lineage, SEM/TEM/EDX, and AFM layer roughness and gate-corner bumps. RMS and bump height are different observables. The suspicious 251 nm label remains literal and unresolved.
5. PACKAGE represents chip attachment to a PCB and two-ended aluminium ultrasonic wire bonds. It requires externally qualified adhesive, pad map, wire geometry, machine settings and bond inspection. Source knob photographs provide no machine recipe. Downstream prepackaged inputs do not count as completing this branch.
6. BASELINE and MOSCAP preserve BG/TG/DG, stack and cohort identity, physical terminal mapping, electrical acquisition and C–V. Baseline N=12, three chips/four systems, is not the separate reliability population. TG curve axes differ from BG; actual ranges require architecture-specific cards.
7. DUAL_TRACE, POSITIVE_STRESS and LONG_TERM are separate populations and time histories. The long-term source says 50 devices and does not unambiguously allocate them per architecture. Actual time and storage custody must be proved independently; changing a day label does not age a sample.
8. NBS and NBTS remain distinct four-kilosecond histories with 500-second intervals; only NBTS adds 80 C. THERMAL measures four source temperatures for one stack before removal and six hours off the hotplate, then advances to the next stack. The ten stacks are not measured simultaneously. Table and device temperatures cannot be equated without qualification.
9. SMALL preserves the separate 1 micrometre width, 500 nm source/drain separation and 100 nm gate device branch. It does not establish a ten-stack nanoscale structure.
10. MATERIAL and COUPLING preserve dielectric comparisons, two buffer thicknesses, four terminal cases and virgin/positive/negative conditions. The S25 caption discrepancy blocks unreviewed wiring. A qualified choice is recorded as an interpretation, never a correction of the source.
11. PAIRS explicitly covers 90 ordered distinct-stack driver/load combinations. PARALLEL varies one to nine distinct loads with S1 as driver; it does not claim every nonempty subset. DG_TUNE and INV_TUNE keep separately controlled gates and distinct netlists. Main Fig4b and SI S31a must not be collapsed into one generic inverter wiring convention.

## Physical versus numerical completion

Every branch includes input verification, safe docking/mounting, qualified controls, raw readout, measured safe electrical state, disconnection, retrieval, condition inspection, archive and cleanup. Service request acknowledgments do not prove measured states. A damaged or consumed coupon cannot silently become a pristine device. Rewiring and remounting invalidate relevant prior receipts.

Analysis is a separate dependency after immutable raw acquisition. The printed ON/OFF ratio, mobility and SS expressions remain conflict gates. No conventional correction is silently substituted. Threshold fits, derivative gain, trip-point definitions, noise margins and uncertainty semantics require approved algorithms. Reported source outcomes are evaluator-only references, never actor rewards or proof of task completion.

## Verification boundaries

The offline verifier uses authored synthetic observations independent of actor event fields. It validates exact control coverage, lineage, card binding, safe-state sequencing, raw-record hashes, source-conflict preservation, serial thermal cooldown, distinct circuit maps and cleanup. Population fixtures are intentionally reduced; they are not 100-device or 200-day replications. The mock has no authentication or production authority and must never be deployed as a hardware safety controller.

No source PDF, prose dump, screenshot, publisher image, raw experimental dataset or private input path belongs in the export. EXPORT_ALLOWLIST.json pins only original contracts, documentation and test code. Parent publication is separate from this local task.
