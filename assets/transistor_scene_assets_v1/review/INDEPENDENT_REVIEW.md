- Five frozen source-task hashes match. Layer, netlist and conflict contracts are exact. All 44 operation IDs are bound once with matching kinds and no execution claim
- Six exact source-station IDs resolve to scene objects, separately from the six semantic asset groups. The initial missing station mapping was corrected. HIGHK, thermal, long-term and destructive-section custody requirements remain explicit

- All ten conflicts remain unresolved qualification gates. Ordinary Fig4 and independent S31 maps differ correctly; VTG1 and VTG2 are distinct. The incomplete parallel topology is rejected
- Independent behavior checks completed 180 pair/netlist lifecycles, rejected wrong types in every receipt-binding field, prevented receipt replay, and rejected all 44 physical-service operations through both entry points after every conflict acknowledgement. The final author suite also passed independently: 75/75
- Fifteen carrier/clamp meshes exactly match the recorded original AFM source and final scene topology/transforms. Two reused families receive zero new unique-asset credit
- Both final GLBs import into a clean Blender 4.3.2 process without external resources, images, textures, required extensions or native layer meshes. The Blend and two GLBs total 10,697,168 bytes, below the 18,000,000-byte budget
- All three final 1600 x 1100, 96-sample CPU renders were directly inspected and matched their receipts. Primary closed-service, no-data, unknown-safe-state and locked-output labels are clear. Small specimen annotations may require the close-up or native scene
- No source pixels or vendor CAD resources are embedded or linked. Geometry comes from the reviewed primitive generator and licensed original reuse; fonts remain editable in Blend and become mesh labels in GLB
