# Source-to-asset boundary

Source: *Rigidly flat-foldable class of lockable origami-inspired metamaterials with topological stiff states*, Nature Communications (2022), DOI10.1038/s41467-022-29484-1. Main article and all37 SI pages were read by a separate source auditor. Twelve supplementary movies were sampled at six timestamps each, not claimed as watched frame-by-frame. This original asset package does not redistribute those materials.

Source-confirmed facts represented narrowly: 0.21 mm dry paperboard thickness; 200 g/m² paperboard label; 240 × 5 mm tensile strip; 2 mm perforation cuts; reported1.4 mm intervals with gap/pitch unresolved; PVA bonding role; smooth aluminum platens; 12.5 kN rated load-cell label; CCD DIC and crosshead displacement roles. Default a=b=15 mm and phi=60° remain design facts, not a claim that authored rectangular coupons or open chains reproduce source panels.

The paper’s six-layer 7×7 physical tessellations, minimum-DoF layer requirements, rigidity layer requirements, source A/O modes, and numerical phase-diagram apparatus are not conflated. No full source unit cell or 7×7 array is drawn. The four-panel accordion is an original open chain with unchanged virtual edge lengths and visibly separated finite-thickness panels. It has no source topology, contact or measured hinges. Static states are pedagogical geometry only.

All apparatus housings, carriers, stops, tabs, mounts, alignment pins, camera body, restraint-ring reference, positions and dimensions are authored. Neither precision of the mesh nor native metric units certifies fit or physical safety. Anchors are metadata selectors and cannot be treated as calibrated robot poses. No external software, instruments or services can be controlled by this package.

Prominent geometry-specific source holds: SI Table1 caption/table color mismatch; N18 listed100° versus formula80°; N4 rigidity text36→30 versus table24→18. Exact manufacturing crease/cut topology, adhesive quantity/cure, finite-thickness contact, source process settings and mixed-mode maps require independent reconstruction and qualification. None is silently filled in.

Original assets/code use the included Apache2.0 license. Publisher article licensing is cited only for provenance and is not used to claim authorship of source research. Blender font data notice is included separately. No prior AFM carrier mesh or other asset was imported; reused-asset credit is zero.

All ten source discrepancy/qualification records are preserved in source_comparison.json, including layer-role distinction, relative-density equation references, energy/threshold angle conventions, N6 modulus interpretation, predeclared first-cycle reference and force-ratio reciprocal ambiguity. Those numerical/analysis roles remain external records; this package implements none of the disputed formulas or policies.
