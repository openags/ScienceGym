# Microsphere white-light microscopy: original review assets

Original ScienceGym assets paired with `microsphere_operations_v2`, based on the accepted original analysis of Wang et al., DOI [10.1038/ncomms1211](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms1211). These are design-review artifacts, not an implemented microscope, robot, digital twin, optical solver, or claim of experimental replication.

## What is included

- Natively compressed, editable Blender scene and GLB with 9 named asset roots, 33 evidence-only anchors, 13 route bindings and 7 stations
- Three genuine Cycles CPU renders, labeled as illustrative scene views
- Source-scale size-context collection, physically separate magnified diagrams, and original protected carrier geometry
- Side-effect-free guarded Python interactions, tests, portable rebuild source and independent audit receipts
- Accepted original-analysis snapshots preserving all 16 qualification gaps, controls, failure branches, custody and source claim distinctions

Open `geometry/microsphere_lab.blend` in Blender, or import `geometry/microsphere_lab.glb` into a glTF viewer. The Blender scene uses meters, a right-handed frame and Z-up; GLB export converts to glTF Y-up. No external textures, linked files, scripts embedded in Blender, animation, physics or drivers are required. Use `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py'` for local guards. External Blender and Python dependencies are described in `provenance.json`.

## Representation boundaries

`LAB_REVIEW` is a wholly original generic facility layout. Every bench, enclosure, carrier, contact envelope and station location is authored and unqualified for physical use. Named support checks establish that the drawn objects touch their drawn supports; they are not mechanical load, friction, collision, grasp or robot-clearance validation.

`SOURCE_SCALE` contains reported diameter size-context spheres (1, 3, 4.74, 10 and 50 micrometers) and two source-size SIL half-ball proxies (0.5 and 2.5 millimeters), within closed carriers. The original 75 × 25 × 1 millimeter slide is an illustrative carrier insert, not a source-specified specimen exterior. Sphere lot, dispersion and optical properties are not qualified. SIL shapes are generic authoring geometry, not an exact optical prescription.

`SCHEMATIC_ENLARGEMENTS` is separate. SIL shapes are displayed at 200× linear size; the 4.74 micrometer sphere is displayed at 40000× linear size. These are illustration scales, not optical magnification. Target icons have no length scale and do not reproduce experimental CAD or a source image. Original grating/pore/track/star category icons are not acquired optical images. Rendering a small feature, a sphere or a star does not demonstrate resolution, contrast, focus, magnification, near-field behavior or measurement uncertainty.

The physical optical station is a conventional white-light microscope within a closed stationary guard. It exposes no lamp, focus, acquisition, beam or vacuum commands. Sphere assembly and target-preparation/SEM services are closed receipt boundaries. There is no biological sample or future biological application in this task.

## Source distinctions retained

- Four illustrated source experimental branches remain separate from text-only size/coating/extensions, proposed array combination, theory/model outputs and speculation
- The source star film composition differs between body/caption; its sphere size remains unreported. Neither is guessed
- Bare and 0.5/2.5 millimeter SIL comparator branches remain visible; source 80×/40× objective conditions remain distinct
- The gold-AAO 50 nm feature claim, reflection 90 nm star-corner claim, 4.17×/approximately 8× reported examples, model outputs and heuristics are source context. None is used as a success threshold or new measurement
- Object, virtual, detector and SEM frames are distinct identities. The displayed frame cards are diagrams, not measured coordinate transformations or literal optical-plane positions
- Preserved uncoated baseline history is required; a coated target is not assumed to restore identically

## Privacy, pairing and immutability

The final native file is separately SDNA-sanitized after the last Blender save. Schema-recognized C strings have canonical post-NUL tails; inactive UI path buffers are reset. Unknown fields are never blanket-cleared. Deep native semantic snapshots prove unchanged meshes, transforms, materials, labels, cameras, units, collections and render settings. Independent receipts redact recovered buffer contents. PNG metadata is removed without changing image pixels.

`scene_core_manifest.json` identifies immutable scene bytes. Reciprocal task/scene references hash selected cores rather than one another, avoiding hash cycles. After acceptance, do not edit the sealed package in place; author a new revision. Rebuilding or resaving invalidates binary hashes and requires a fresh sanitation, equivalence, render and independent-review pass.
