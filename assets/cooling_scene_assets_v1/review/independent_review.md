## Source fidelity and authored choices

Reported emitter, insulation, ring, film, shield, aperture, reflector and track-stock
dimensions are retained separately from invented fit dimensions. The 52 mm PE
support height, 9.3 mm pedestal, 3.5 mm shield adapter, upper-insulation heater
recess, installation planes/gaps, film trim, mounts, straight track and instrument
geometry are explicitly authored. None supplies an unknown source tolerance.

The **100/150 mm reflector-height ambiguity remains unresolved**. A 150 mm display
pose does not resolve the reference-plane/version conflict. Source sensor position
schematics do exist; the six stored probe poses are not an installation coordinate
map, and mounting tolerances remain unknown.

Four paired-device membranes retain physical 16 micrometre thickness. The lower
and upper handling-film components, including the lower torn representation, use
50x thickness only: 0.8 mm display thickness, with no XY enlargement. This cannot
establish a feasible physical grasp. Lower damage preserves component identity;
replacement increments that component's revision, without implementing full task
assembly lineage or immutable scientific records.

## Findings corrected before acceptance

The carrier nameplate now follows the semantic state. The torn representation
carries the same current film identity. The emitter/lower-film overlap was removed
using documented authored offsets, with an authored heater-clearance recess in the
upper foam. Counts, exclusions, event bindings and torn-variant display-scale
wording were synchronized. Lid affordances now correctly describe shared paired
motion rather than an independent per-device API.

## Pixel evidence and remaining limits

The final images clearly show the paired device identities, carrier handles and
clamps, exploded-film scale note, metric ruler, rear inspection coupon, six-probe
rack, demo controls and source-boundary warnings. They are coherent original
renders and contain no source-paper figure pixels.

These are canonical baseline views. Interior assembled parts and much of the
angle fixture are occluded; some small captions require zoom or metadata lookup.
Moving, torn and replacement states were checked in native binding tests rather
than alternate-state render pixels. The evidence does not visually certify every
local frame, access direction or contact region.

The straight track is an authored preview, controls are coupled where documented,
and the GLBs are static. There is no thermal/optical/electrical model, DAQ, real
sensor stream, heater output, membrane mechanics, collision/contact backend,
qualified gripper interface or robot-feasibility test. Full experimental stations,
qualified geometry, calibration, source coordinates and execution receipts remain
future work.

procedural materials and original renders. No publisher PDF, source photograph,
figure pixels, vendor CAD, logo or external texture is included. The built-in font
is unpacked, and attribution/license notices are supplied. This is a provenance
and resource review, not a legal certification.

local demo-state copies. Exact ZIP members, hashes, integrity and archive size are
